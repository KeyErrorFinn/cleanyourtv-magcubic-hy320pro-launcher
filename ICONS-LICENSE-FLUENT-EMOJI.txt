The seasonal skins' pictures in app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/ are from Microsoft's Fluent Emoji 3D
(https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji), cropped to their visible area and resized to 128 px WebP:

- Halloween: halloween_bat, halloween_ghost, halloween_pumpkin_lit and halloween_pumpkin_dark (Bat, Ghost,
  Jack-o-lantern). The lit and dark pumpkins are the same picture with the carved face recoloured as a candle-lit
  and an unlit face. These four are taken from the upstream CleanYourTV launcher.
- Christmas: season_christmas_gift, season_christmas_tree, season_christmas_snowman (Wrapped gift, Christmas tree,
  Snowman).
- New Year: season_newyear_glasses, season_newyear_sparkles, season_newyear_popper (Clinking glasses, Sparkles,
  Party popper).
- Easter: season_easter_rabbit, season_easter_chick, season_easter_tulip (Rabbit face, Hatching chick, Tulip).
- Valentine's Day: season_valentine_letter, season_valentine_heart, season_valentine_rose (Love letter, Red heart,
  Rose).
- St. Patrick's Day: season_patrick_rainbow, season_patrick_clover, season_patrick_coin (Rainbow, Four leaf
  clover, Coin).
